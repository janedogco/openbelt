Questions

What is OpenBelt?

The open-source tool belt for you and your agents. Each tool gets a URL, data, jobs and sign-in, and works from any agent.

Which agents work with it?

Any MCP client: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, opencode and more. Add one address and sign in.

Do my agents see my keys?

No. You connect a service once. Apps and agents call it through OpenBelt and never get the key.

Where do my apps run?

On OpenBelt, at your-workspace.openbelt.app/your-app. Or on your own server with Docker.

How does my agent know an app works?

It opens each change in a real browser and checks it before it goes live.

Can I take my apps with me?

Yes. An app is a folder of plain files your agent can read and export.
