# Log work

Add one document to the journal app's `entries`: `{ text, day }`.

- `text`: 1-3 plain sentences in the past tense: what you did, the result, and anything left over. No markdown.
- `day`: today in the person's timezone, as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

One entry per piece of work, not one per step. The person skims these at the end of the day, so lead with the outcome.
